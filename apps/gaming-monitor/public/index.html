Quest connection test

Quest support: unverified

See what actually arrives.

  1. In Meta Horizon, open your profile/settings and find connected accounts or Discord account linking. Availability and labels can vary by account, region and app version.
  2. Use the official Meta/Discord consent flow to connect your accounts and enable activity sharing. Never supply a Discord user token.
  3. In Discord, enable activity sharing in Activity Privacy. Join the private server configured for this monitor, and appear online.
  4. Switch this dashboard to Discord and connect. Record a baseline with the Quest idle and all PC games closed.
  5. Launch a standalone Quest game, such as Walkabout Mini Golf. Wait for presence; compare the observations below. Stop the game, then repeat with a second title.

A new gaming activity is an observation, not proof of Quest origin. Check the official account connection, control for PC activity and compare start/stop events. The current Discord fields cannot independently verify device attribution, so this monitor keeps platform Unknown. No activity may mean the integration does not expose that title.
